# Offsets and Blends

## Offsets
An offset surface is generated by moving every point of a base surface by a constant distance $d$ along its normal vector.
$$ S_{offset}(u, v) = S(u, v) + d \cdot \vec{n}(u, v) $$
Offsetting is highly non-linear. An offset of a simple spline may have self-intersections (swallowtails) if the offset distance exceeds the local radius of curvature. In [3D printing](../applications/3d-printing.md), offsetting algorithms are used in slicers to generate wall perimeters (shelling).

## Blends (Fillets and Chamfers)
Blending creates a smooth transition between two adjacent surfaces. This reduces stress concentrations in mechanical parts and eliminates sharp corners that can cause nozzle collisions or over-extrusion artifacts in FDM printing.
