# Surface Modeling

## Guide

This folder is dedicated to **Surface Modeling**. It covers essential topics such as B-Splines, NURBS, Offsets and Blends, and more.

## Directory Map

* [B-Splines](b-splines.md) - Basis splines providing local control for curve and surface representation.
* [NURBS](nurbs.md) - Non-Uniform Rational B-Splines for representing exact conic sections and free-form surfaces.
* [Offsets and Blends](offsets-blends.md) - Creating parallel surfaces and smooth transitions between intersecting geometries.
* [Sweeps and Generalized Cylinders](sweeps.md) - Generating 3D volumes by sweeping a 2D cross-section along a trajectory.
