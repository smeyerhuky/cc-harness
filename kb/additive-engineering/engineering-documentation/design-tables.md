Rather than designing multiple independent files for slightly varying parts (e.g., a set of screwdrivers of different lengths, or drones with varying rotor counts), designers use configurations.

Configurations allow a single CAD file to store multiple geometric variations. These are often driven by **Equations** and **Design Tables**. 

By linking sketch dimensions and feature states (suppressed vs. unsuppressed) to a central spreadsheet (design table), the designer can rapidly generate entire product lines. When a master dimension is updated, the equations ensure that the geometric proportions of the model update automatically, preventing manual rework.
