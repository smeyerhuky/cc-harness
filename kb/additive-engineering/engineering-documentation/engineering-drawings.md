Engineering drawings act as the canonical contract between the designer and the manufacturer. Once a 3D model is complete, it is projected onto a 2D sheet to convey manufacturing instructions.

Best practices for engineering drawings include:
- **Orthographic Projections:** Providing clear front, top, and right-side views.
- **Tolerancing:** Defining the acceptable variance in dimensions to ensure parts fit together in reality.
- **Material Specifications:** Clearly indicating the physical material required.
- **Bill of Materials (BOM):** For assemblies, generating a table that lists all constituent parts, quantities, and descriptions, often accompanied by an exploded view.
