# Triply Periodic Minimal Surfaces (TPMS)

TPMS are used for advanced infill, heat exchangers, and medical implants. They are defined implicitly by $f(x,y,z) = 0$.

*   **The Gyroid Equation**: $\sin(x)\cos(y) + \sin(y)\cos(z) + \sin(z)\cos(x) = 0$
*   Because the Mean Curvature is zero everywhere, they have no sharp corners or stress concentrators, providing a near-perfect strength-to-weight ratio and allowing perfectly continuous extrusion paths without retractions.
