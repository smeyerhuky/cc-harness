# Constructive Geometry

Constructive geometry relies on combining primitive geometric shapes using boolean operations.

## Primitives and Operations
* **Primitives**: The fundamental building blocks include spheres, cubes, cylinders, and polyhedrons.
* **Boolean Operations**: 
  * **Union**: Combines multiple shapes into a single solid.
  * **Difference**: Subtracts one shape from another, useful for creating holes or hollow structures.
  * **[Intersection](../geometric-algorithms/intersection.md)**: Retains only the overlapping volume of multiple shapes.
* **Minkowski Sum**: An operation that traces the outline of one shape along the surface of another, commonly used for rounding edges and creating offsets.
* **Spatial Reasoning**: Complex [science models](../applications/science-models.md) can be decomposed into a sequence of simple geometric transformations and boolean operations.
