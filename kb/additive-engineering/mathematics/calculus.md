# Calculus

Calculus concepts are applied in 3D modeling to analyze curves, surfaces, and volumes, particularly when creating complex swept geometries or optimizing shapes.

## Core Applications
* **Derivatives and Tangents**: The derivative of a function provides the slope of the tangent line. This is crucial for calculating surface normals and ensuring smooth transitions (blends) between parametric curves.
* **Integrals and Volume**: Definite integrals are used to calculate the area under a curve or the volume of a solid of revolution. When a 2D profile is rotated computationally, it essentially performs a volume integration.
* **Optimization**: Finding local maxima and minima helps in determining bounding boxes and optimizing material usage in [3D printing](../applications/3d-printing.md).
