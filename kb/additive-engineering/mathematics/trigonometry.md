# Trigonometry and Analytic [Geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md)

Trigonometry and analytic [geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md) form the mathematical foundation for defining shapes and transformations in 3D space. 

## Key Concepts
* **Coordinate Systems**: Cartesian coordinates (X, Y, Z) are used to position vertices and models. Polar or cylindrical coordinates are often calculated using sine and cosine functions.
* **Transformations**: Rotations around axes depend heavily on trigonometric functions. A rotation by an angle $\theta$ around the Z-axis modifies X and Y coordinates according to:
  $x' = x \cos(\theta) - y \sin(\theta)$
  $y' = x \sin(\theta) + y \cos(\theta)$
* **Conic Sections**: Circles, ellipses, parabolas, and hyperbolas can be defined analytically and parameterized for 3D extrusion. In computational [geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md), these are often approximated by polygons with a defined number of facets ($fn$).
* **Vectors**: Directional movements and normal vectors for surfaces are calculated using trigonometric relationships, ensuring accurate [textual modeling](../programming/textual-modeling.md).
