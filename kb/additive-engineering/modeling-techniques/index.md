# Modeling Techniques

## Guide

This folder is dedicated to **Modeling Techniques**. It covers essential topics such as 2D to 3D Extrusion, Advanced Surfacing and Features, Assemblies and Motion, and more.

## Directory Map

* [2D to 3D Extrusion](2d-to-3d-extrusion.md) - Core concept from CodeSolutions
* [Advanced Surfacing and Features](advanced-surfacing.md) - Generating complex, organic 3D geometries.
* [Assemblies and Motion](assemblies-and-motion.md) - Combining individual CAD parts into functional, movable mechanisms.
* [Elementary Features](elementary-features.md) - Core 3D operations for generating and modifying primitive volumes.
* [Patterns and Symmetry](patterns-and-symmetry.md) - Leveraging repetition and symmetry to simplify CAD design.
* [Primitives and Booleans](primitives-and-booleans.md) - Core concept from Intro to OpenSCAD / CodeSolutions
* [Sketches and Constraints](sketches-and-constraints.md) - The foundation of parametric 3D modeling, defining 2D profiles using geometric constraints.
