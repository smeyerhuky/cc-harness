An assembly is a CAD environment where multiple distinct parts are brought together to form a complex system. 

The positional relationship between parts is governed by **Mates** (or Constraints). Mates define the degrees of freedom allowed between two components (e.g., locking them rigidly, allowing rotation along an axis, or allowing planar sliding).

Assemblies also allow for the modeling of mechanical kinematics, such as:
- **Gear Ratios and Motion:** Defining the rotational relationship between meshed gears.
- **Circular-to-Linear Motion:** Simulating mechanisms like rack-and-pinions or cams.
- **Exploded Views:** Visually separating the components of an assembly along their axes of insertion to demonstrate how the system is put together.
