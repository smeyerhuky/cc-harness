Parametric 3D modeling relies on fully defining 2D profiles before generating 3D features. This requires an understanding of reference planes (e.g., front, top, right) and geometric constraints.

A sketch is considered "fully defined" when its [geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md), position, and orientation are completely locked in space using a combination of:
1. **Dimensions:** Explicit length, angle, or radii values.
2. **Relationships/Constraints:** Geometric rules applied between entities (e.g., horizontal, vertical, tangent, coincident, parallel).

Advanced sketching often requires using **splines** for freeform, continuous curves or tracing over imported **sketch pictures** for [reverse engineering](../design-methodology/reverse-engineering.md) real-world objects.
