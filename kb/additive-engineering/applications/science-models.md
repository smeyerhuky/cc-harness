# Scientific Modeling

Computational 3D modeling is used to visualize abstract scientific concepts across physics, biology, and geology.

## Applications
* **Physics and Mechanics**: Simulating simple machines, calculating moments of inertia, and visualizing waveforms or the Doppler shift.
* **Geology**: Creating models of geological strata and topographic maps.
* **Chemistry**: Modeling molecular structures, crystal lattices, and trusses using [constructive geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md).
* **Iterative Experimentation**: Because the models are driven by [parameterization](../programming/parameterization.md), variables can be altered to run "math experiments", changing wave frequencies, gear ratios, or atomic radii to instantly see the structural outcome.
