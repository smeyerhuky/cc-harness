# Processes

## Guide

This folder is dedicated to **Processes**. It covers essential topics such as Injection Molding, Layered and Additive Manufacturing, Metal Cutting, and more.

## Directory Map

* [Injection Molding](injection-molding.md) - Process of injecting molten polymer into a mold cavity to form a part.
* [Layered and Additive Manufacturing](layered-manufacturing.md) - Principles, mechanisms, and constraints of additive manufacturing processes like Fused Deposition Modeling (FDM).
* [Metal Cutting](metal-cutting.md) - Material removal processes using a cutting tool to shear away material in the form of chips.
* [Thermoforming](thermoforming.md) - Process of heating a thermoplastic sheet and forming it over a mold.
