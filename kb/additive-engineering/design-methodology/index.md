# Design Methodology

## Guide

This folder is dedicated to **Design Methodology**. It covers essential topics such as Feature Mapping, Geometric Decomposition, Parametric Best Practices, and more.

## Directory Map

* [Feature Mapping](feature-mapping.md) - Matching specific physical geometries to the optimal CAD modeling commands.
* [Geometric Decomposition](geometric-decomposition.md) - Breaking down a complex design into smaller, manageable subcomponents.
* [Parametric Best Practices](parametric-best-practices.md) - Core concept from Intro to OpenSCAD
* [Reverse Engineering](reverse-engineering.md) - The process of designing a 3D model solely by analyzing external features and pictures of an existing object.
