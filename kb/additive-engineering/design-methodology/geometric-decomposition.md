Geometric decomposition is the strategic process of breaking a complex object down into simpler, distinct subcomponents.

When modeling advanced assemblies, attempting to build the entire [geometry](../mathematics/geometry.md) simultaneously is inefficient and prone to error. Instead, the object should be decomposed visually (often with the aid of reference pictures). Each subcomponent can then be individually analyzed and modeled before being assembled into the final product.

This approach significantly simplifies the cognitive load of 3D modeling and naturally leads to more robust, modular designs. It is a critical precursor to [Feature Mapping](feature_mapping.md), where each isolated subcomponent is matched with the most appropriate CAD command.
