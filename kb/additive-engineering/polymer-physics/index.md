# Polymer Physics

## Guide

This folder is dedicated to **Polymer Physics**. It covers topics including Non-Newtonian Melts & Die Swell, Reptation Theory, Transient Heat Equations & Residual Stress.

## Directory Map

* [Non-Newtonian Melts & Die Swell](non-newtonian-melts.md) - Viscoelastic behavior of polymers under shear stress.
* [Reptation Theory](reptation-theory.md) - The physics of polymer chains slithering across layers to create strong welds.
* [Transient Heat Equations & Residual Stress](transient-heat-equations.md) - How thermal gradients induce warping and delamination.
