        The rules, as built

        Your decisions, the netcode that makes live play work, and what is still open.

        Decided

        • Leaving the tab or closing it pauses both games at once, and both boards are hidden while paused.
        • The player who stayed gets a popover with the reason, a 2:00 timer they can extend at any time, and Leave, which ends the match as no contest (configurable).
        • Each player has a pause budget (2). A connection loss caught by missed heartbeats doesn't use it, up to 3 free reconnects.
        • With no pauses left, leaving doesn't pause: the match keeps running and the player has 15 s to return or forfeit.
        • If both players are away, the session ends after 5:00.
        • Returning players see how long they were gone and how many pauses they have left.
        • Phones are supported: on-screen controls, a screen wake lock, and heartbeats as the source of truth.

        Live netcode

        Piece of the designHow it works here
        Your own boardRuns locally at 60 ticks a second with DAS and ARR, so moves never wait on the network.
        Opponent's boardPosition updates 15 times a second plus a snapshot on every lock, relayed by the DO.
        Hidden next piecesThe DO keeps the seed and deals one bag of seven pieces at a time. Both players get the same pieces and gems.
        Speed-upEvery 15 s of active play on the DO's clock, so both players speed up together and pauses don't count.
        ShowdownsAnnounced 5 s ahead. 1:00: double garbage for 15 s. 2:30: sudden death, speed +4 and garbage ×2 until someone tops out. The DO applies the ×2 when it routes attacks.
        GarbageEach routed attack gets an id. Clients acknowledge ids in their next update, and on rejoin the DO resends anything unacknowledged.
        Pause timerA DO alarm. Extend reschedules it. Both screens show the DO's deadline.
        What happenedHow the Match DO finds outUses a pause?
        Switched tab or appvisibilitychange → client sends awayYes
        Closed the tabWebSocket closes → webSocketCloseYes
        Lost Wi-Fi, crash, sleepNo heartbeat for 5 sNo, up to 3 times

        Still open

        1. Is 15 s of grace enough on a phone, where a call or a notification can take longer than that?
        2. Should power-ups and showdowns be switched off for ranked matches?
        3. When a player closes the tab, the piece in their hand is dealt again on rejoin. Is that fair, or should it be kept?
        4. Should a player who has run out of free reconnects be told before they drop again?
