        The pause rule

        What you decided, how the build would detect each case, and what is still open.

        • Leaving the tab or closing it pauses both games at once.
        • The player who stayed gets a popover that says why the game is paused and asks: wait or leave.
        • The wait timer defaults to 2:00. The waiting player can extend it at any time while waiting.
        What happenedHow the Match DO finds outHow fast
        Switched tab or appBrowser fires visibilitychange; the client sends awayInstant
        Closed the tabThe WebSocket closes and webSocketClose runs in the DO. pagehide can send a last away first.Usually instant
        Crash, sleep, lost Wi-FiNo close arrives. The DO notices missing heartbeats.About 5 s. The other player keeps playing until then.

        The countdown lives in the Match DO as an alarm, so both screens show the same deadline and no browser clock can bend it. Extend reschedules the alarm. Rejoining after a closed tab uses a join token kept in the browser; the DO answers with the current state.

        Open questions

        1. Does Leave give the waiting player a win, or end as no contest? If it's a win, should it unlock only after a short wait, say 15 s, so a quick tab switch isn't punished at once?
        2. Pause abuse: a losing player can leave to break the other's rhythm or buy thinking time. Options: a pause budget (2 per match), and hiding both boards while paused. This demo blurs them.
        3. Cap the total pause, for example 10:00, so abandoned matches get cleaned up.
        4. Both players away at once: end as no contest?
        5. On phones, switching apps, locking the screen and incoming calls all count as leaving. Is 2:00 enough there?
        6. Does the returning player see how long they were gone, and how close they came to forfeiting?
