## Surprises and what they changed

- **"Latest" had two conflicts.** On 2026-09-30, TypeScript 7.0.2 and Vitest 5.0.2 were
  released, but `typescript-eslint` 8.71 accepts TypeScript `<6.1` and
  `@cloudflare/vitest-pool-workers` 0.22 requires Vitest `^4.1` (from `npm view … peerDependencies`).
  The stack pins TypeScript 6.0.3 and Vitest 4.1.11 as documented exceptions.
- **The demo's piece colours were the Tetris guideline's.** Found while writing the UI language.
  The PRD rules out Tetris trade dress, so v1 gets a new palette with pattern marks.
- **The demo's gravity used `Math.pow`**, which is not guaranteed bit-identical across JavaScript
  engines. The design switches to a fixed-point table (`GD-TICKET-008`).
- **Six design commitments had no carrier** until the coverage audit, which is its purpose;
  each is now an item.
