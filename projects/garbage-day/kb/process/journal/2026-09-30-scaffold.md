## Surprises and what they changed

- **"Latest" and "a day old" collide on release day.** Wrangler 4.144.0 and
  `@cloudflare/vite-plugin` 1.62.2 were published at 21:12 and 21:22 UTC the evening before, and
  `@cloudflare/workers-types` 5.20260930.1 at 01:29 UTC, all under the workspace's
  `minimumReleaseAge` of 1440 minutes. They are not needed until the app shell, so they wait for
  [`GD-TICKET-011`](../backlog/GD-TICKET-011.md). Stack and CI now defines "latest" as the newest
  release at least a day old.
- **`@vitejs/plugin-react` 6 no longer runs Babel.** The React Compiler goes through
  `@rolldown/plugin-babel` (0.2.4) with `@babel/core` 8.0.6 and the plugin's
  `reactCompilerPreset()`. The native oxc route exists but is marked experimental.
- **ESLint's project service needs every linted file inside a tsconfig.** The first lint failed
  with "vitest.config.ts was not found by the project service"; a root `tsconfig.json` that holds
  only the root Vitest config fixed it, and the typecheck now includes it.
- **Knip flagged `react`, `react-dom` and `@types/react` in `ui`** as unused development
  dependencies, although the tests need them; it also reported the entry patterns first written
  in `knip.json` as redundant. `knip.json` is now two lines of ignores.
- **No project references.** Packages export their TypeScript source, so `tsc -b` (which needs
  every package to emit) gave way to `tsc --noEmit -p` per package.
