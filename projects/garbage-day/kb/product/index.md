# Product — Garbage Day

The spec: what Garbage Day is, for whom, what v1 must do, and the rules it plays by. The PRD is
the entry point; the other three files hold the numbers and interaction details its acceptance
criteria cite.

* [Product requirements (PRD)](prd.md) - What it is, for whom, goals, v1 scope, user stories with acceptance criteria, out of scope, open questions
* [Game rules](game-rules.md) - The board, pieces, timing, attack table, garbage, speed curve, power-ups and showdowns, with their v1 values
* [Pause and presence rules](pause-and-presence.md) - What happens when a player leaves, closes the tab, loses the connection or returns
* [Controls and layout](controls-and-layout.md) - Keyboard, touch gestures, on-screen controls, and how the screen is used on desktop and phone
